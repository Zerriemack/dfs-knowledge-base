# Mass Multi-Entry Strategy

Mass multi-entry uses many lineups to express a portfolio of slate outcomes. Volume does not replace research. It increases the need for exposure limits, construction rules, and post-slate review.

## Set rules before generating lineups

Choose a player pool, maximum exposures, stacking requirements, and a rule for correlated opponents. These settings should follow your slate read. A broad player pool without role standards can create lineups that technically differ but share no coherent path.

## Manage portfolio risk

Exposure is a statement about how much a result affects the whole entry set. If a value play is uncertain, avoid allowing every lineup to depend on that player. Track game-stack exposure as well as player exposure, because concentrated game assumptions can drive the portfolio.

Use an optimizer to enforce constraints, then inspect the output manually.
