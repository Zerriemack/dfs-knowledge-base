# Field Size and Payout Structure

The contest lobby tells you how many opponents you face and how prizes are distributed. Those details should affect lineup construction before lock.

## Read the payout shape

Flatter payouts place more value on consistent finishing positions. Top-heavy payouts place more value on a lineup that can reach the highest range of outcomes. A contest with a large field and a major first-place prize usually increases the cost of a duplicated, median-oriented roster.

## Match the lineup to the field

Smaller tournaments can reward a strong projection with modest differentiation. Larger fields make lineup uniqueness and ceiling more relevant. Do not treat field size as a command to fade every popular player. Instead, consider whether the full lineup has a credible path to stand apart.

Review the entry fee, total entries, first-place payout, and minimum-cash level before committing.
