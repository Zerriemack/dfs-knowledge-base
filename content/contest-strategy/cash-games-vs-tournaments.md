# Cash Games vs. Tournaments

Cash games reward clearing a payout line. Tournaments reward a lineup that can separate from a larger field. The difference changes how much uncertainty a roster should accept.

## Cash-game target

Double-ups and head-to-heads usually favor stable roles, strong median projections, and salary values with secure playing time. A popular player can be useful when fading that player adds risk without creating a larger payout. Review late news, but avoid changing a sound build to chase uniqueness.

## Tournament target

Tournament payouts are commonly concentrated near the top. Ceiling, correlation, and a differentiated construction matter more because a merely solid score may have limited value. That does not mean selecting weak players; it means giving a defensible roster a route to an uncommon high-end outcome.

| Format | Primary lineup question |
| --- | --- |
| Cash | Can this roster clear the payout line? |
| Tournament | Can this roster contend near the top? |
