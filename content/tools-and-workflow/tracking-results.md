# Tracking Results

Tracking results turns individual slates into a record of decisions. The purpose is to evaluate process over a useful sample, not to treat a short streak as proof of a strategy.

## Record the contest context

For each entry, save contest type, field size, entry fee, payout structure, lineup construction, and late-news decisions. Add a short note about the original slate read. This lets you compare similar formats rather than mixing cash entries with large-field tournaments.

## Review decision quality

Look for recurring errors such as entering the wrong format, overconcentrating a portfolio, missing news, or using unsupported value. Track outcomes, but also review whether the process made sense with the information available at lock.

> A useful log explains the decision before it explains the result.
