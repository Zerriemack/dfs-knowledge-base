# Lineup Review

A final lineup review checks whether each roster spot supports the same contest objective. It is a safeguard against errors and disconnected decisions, not a reason to remake every lineup near lock.

## Pre-lock checklist

1. Confirm salary, roster eligibility, and player status.
2. Check that the lineup matches the selected contest format.
3. Identify the game environment and ceiling path.
4. Review ownership and duplication at the lineup level.
5. Leave later-starting players in flexible positions when platform rules allow.

## Preserve the decision record

Write down the main assumptions behind the build. After the slate, separate a sound decision that had a poor outcome from a decision made with stale news or an unsupported role. The preserved [[embedded-files|lineup checklist PDF]] can support this review.
