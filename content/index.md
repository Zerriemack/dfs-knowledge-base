# Daily Fantasy Sports Knowledge Base

This knowledge base organizes a repeatable approach to daily fantasy sports research and lineup decisions. It is written for players who want to move from a slate question to a documented process instead of reacting to one result.

Start by choosing the contest you plan to enter, then build a player read and slate view. Use lineup construction to turn those inputs into a roster, and use the workflow section to review decisions after the slate. DFS includes variance, so no process guarantees a result.

## Start Here

1. [[contest-strategy/index|Choose a contest format]] and its payout target.
2. [[player-research/index|Research roles and projections]].
3. [[slate-analysis/index|Identify the slate conditions]] that change player ranges and ownership.
4. [[lineup-construction/index|Build a lineup plan]] with salary, correlation, and leverage in mind.
5. [[tools-and-workflow/index|Review and track the process]] after entries are submitted.
