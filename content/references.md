# References

This knowledge base explains process and strategy rather than presenting sourced performance claims. The sources below are used for platform rules, lineup mechanics, and football research context. Pages without a source link are general DFS explanations and should receive manual source review before adding factual claims or numerical findings.

- [DraftKings Help Center](https://help.draftkings.com/hc/en-us): Used as general platform context in [[contest-strategy/index|Contest Strategy]], [[lineup-construction/late-swap-process|Late Swap Process]], and [[tools-and-workflow/lineup-review|Lineup Review]].
