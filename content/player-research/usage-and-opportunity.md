# Usage and Opportunity

Opportunity is the foundation of a player read. For NFL DFS, useful indicators include routes, carries, targets, snaps, and red-zone involvement. They show how a player can accumulate fantasy scoring chances.

## Separate role from box-score noise

A recent fantasy score can be driven by a long touchdown or an unusual game script. Review the underlying role before assuming the result will repeat. A player who remains on the field but loses targets may need a different explanation than a player whose snap share fell.

## Read opportunity with team context

Team pace, injury replacements, and expected game flow can change volume. A backup stepping into a starter's workload deserves more attention than a low-priced player whose role remains limited. Confirm the depth chart before treating reported opportunity as current.
