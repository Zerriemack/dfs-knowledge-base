# Ceiling and Floor

Floor describes a player's lower-end plausible outcome; ceiling describes the high-end outcome that can change a contest. Neither label should be separated from role, salary, and contest type.

## Find the ceiling path

Ceiling often comes from concentrated opportunity, efficient scoring, or a game script that creates extra volume. A receiver with deep targets and red-zone usage may have a wider range than a short-area option. The question is whether that upside is plausible at the player's price.

## Use floor in context

Stable playing time and recurring touches can improve a floor, but a player can still fail when the team underperforms. Cash-game builds may favor reliable roles. Tournament builds can accept greater volatility when the salary and correlation create a stronger top-end path.
