# Injuries and Depth Charts

Injury news can change a slate faster than any prebuilt model. The useful response is to identify the replacement role, confirm availability, and reassess salary and ownership rather than assuming every backup receives the same workload.

## Confirm the news

Check the latest official status, expected starter, and any reported limitation. An inactive player creates a different decision from a player who is active but expected to share work. Depth charts are a guide, so compare them with recent usage and coaching reports.

## Trace the opportunity

When a starter is out, opportunity may flow to one replacement, several teammates, or a different play-calling approach. Consider who gains snaps, targets, carries, red-zone chances, and salary flexibility. Then anticipate whether the field will react strongly enough to create ownership pressure.
