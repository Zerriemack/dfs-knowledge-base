# Vegas Lines and Implied Totals

The spread and game total summarize a market expectation about scoring and competitiveness. They offer a starting point for identifying games that may support fantasy production, not a forecast of individual player results.

## Translate the line carefully

An implied team total can help compare expected scoring environments. It does not tell you which player scores, how opportunity is distributed, or whether the game follows the expected script. Pair the total with target concentration, rushing roles, and pricing.

## Look for changes

Movement in a line can reflect new information, but it may also be noise. Note meaningful changes and investigate the cause before adjusting a player pool. Injury news, weather, and quarterback status can affect the interpretation.
