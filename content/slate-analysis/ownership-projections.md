# Ownership Projections

Ownership projections estimate how often players may appear in lineups. They are most useful as a description of field behavior, not as an instruction to fade every popular option.

## Identify the reason for popularity

Players draw ownership because of price, role, recent news, projected points, or game environment. Knowing the reason helps determine whether the field is reacting rationally. A popular replacement with an expanded role may be difficult to avoid; a popular player with a fragile assumption may invite a pivot.

## Evaluate at lineup level

Ownership matters most in combination. Several popular values, a common stack, and full salary usage can produce a duplicated roster. One popular player can be fine if the rest of the lineup has a coherent source of differentiation.
