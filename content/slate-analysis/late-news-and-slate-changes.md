# Late News and Slate Changes

Late information can change player roles, game expectations, and ownership in a short window. Prepare a response before news arrives so the decision remains deliberate.

## Make a contingency list

For questionable players, list the likely replacement and teammates who could gain opportunity. Record salary pivots at the same position and flexible slots. This short list reduces rushed choices when an inactive report appears.

## Rebuild the reasoning

When news breaks, update role first, then projection, salary allocation, ownership, and correlation. Do not assume the replacement is automatically the best play. The new role may be shared or priced into the slate.
